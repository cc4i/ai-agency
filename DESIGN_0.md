🌳 The Core Concept: "The AI Agency"
The new design reframes the metaphor: the user is the "Creative Director", but they are no longer using a single "assistant." Instead, they are directing an entire "AI Agency" composed of specialist agents.



The User's Role: The Creative Director.


The AI's Role: The user's single point of contact is the "Executive Producer" (or "Account Manager"), powered by Gemini Live.



The Interaction: The user directs their Producer through a natural, streaming conversation. The Producer, in turn, manages and delegates tasks to its team of specialist agents (like a Strategy Agent, Art Director, Video Producer, etc.).




The Goal: To launch the "Aura" Smart Sneaker campaign.

Phase 1: The "Handoff" & Agency Planning
This phase establishes the new agentic workflow, moving from a simple start to an explicit, user-approved plan.

The Welcome (The "Handoff")


Headline: "Welcome, Creative Director." 


Visual: A persistent, glowing microphone icon.


Call to Action: "Click the mic and say 'Let's get started'." 


User Action (Voice In): "Let's get started." 


Gemini Live (Producer): "Welcome. I'm your Executive Producer. Our first project is the 'Aura' Smart Sneaker launch. I've pulled up the initial sketch. To start, I need to build out the core marketing strategy. Shall I proceed?" 



User Action (Voice In): "Yes, go ahead." 


The "Planning & Reasoning" Step (Agentic Tweak)

Instead of immediately executing, the Producer first presents its "chain of thought" as a formal plan.



Gemini Live (Producer): "Great. To launch the 'Aura' Smart Sneaker, I've broken the project into 5 phases: 

First, our Strategy Agent [Gemini Pro] will define the personas and slogans. 

Then, our Art Director [Imagen] will create the hero image. 

After that, our Video Producer [Veo] will handle the social clip. 

Simultaneously, our Audio Team [Lyria] will compose the jingle and podcast ad. 

Finally, our Web Dev Agent [Gemini Code Assist] will build the landing page. 


Gemini Live (Producer): "This plan is now in your Project Brief. Shall I task the Strategy Agent to begin?" 

User Action (Voice In): "Yes, task the Strategy Agent."

Phase 2: The "Agency Hub" (Directed Interaction)
The user is in a continuous Gemini Live session, directing their Producer. The Producer, in turn, delegates tasks, manages proactive collaboration between its agents, and performs internal critiques.

Step 1: Strategy (Strategy Agent - Gemini Pro)


Gemini Live (Producer): "Okay, I've tasked our Strategy Agent [Gemini Pro] with analyzing the sketch.  (a short 'thinking' animation plays) ... It's generated three key customer personas and five potential slogans. They are on your screen now." 



Workspace: The app displays the text output.

User (Voice In): "I like slogan number three: 'Run on light.' Let's use that one." 

Step 2: Art (Art Director Agent - Imagen)


Gemini Live (Producer): "Excellent choice. Now, I'm sending this slogan to our Art Director Agent [Imagen] to generate the hero image." 



Workspace: Displays four photorealistic images generated by Imagen.


User (Voice In): "The one on the top right is perfect." 


Step 3: Proactive Collaboration (Agentic Tweak)

The agents now demonstrate parallel work and context sharing, rather than waiting for the user's next command.



Gemini Live (Producer): "Got it. I've added that to the project brief. Our Video Producer Agent [Veo] and Web Dev Agent [Gemini Code Assist] have already been notified and are using that image as their style reference. 


Gemini Live (Producer): "While they work, our Audio Agent [Lyria] has a proactive suggestion: based on the 'Tokyo neon' theme and the 'Run on light' slogan, it recommends an 'uplifting, futuristic, electronic beat'. Do you want to hear a sample?" 

Step 4: Internal Critique Loop (Agentic Tweak)

The Producer reviews the work from its agents before presenting it, demonstrating autonomous reasoning and self-correction.


User (Voice In): "Yes, play the sample... That's great. Now, show me the clip from the Video Producer."


Gemini Live (Producer): "Okay, our Video Producer Agent [Veo] has a first pass... (video plays)...Hmm, I'm analyzing it against our brief. The 'Tokyo neon' theme is strong, but it doesn't clearly show the 'glowing sole'. I'm sending it back to the agent with instructions for a 2-second close-up. 


(Processing animation) 


Gemini Live (Producer): "Okay, the revision is complete. Here is the new version." 

Step 5: Final Asset Generation (All Agents)

User (Voice In): "That's perfect. Let's get the audio and the landing page finalized."


Gemini Live (Producer): "On it. Our Audio Team has composed the custom jingle with Lyria , generated the TTS voiceover for the podcast ad , and prepared the Chirp transcription for the global launch.




Gemini Live (Producer): "Simultaneously, our Web Dev Agent [Gemini Code Assist] has generated the code for the 'Coming Soon' page, using our hero image and slogan. The code is generated. I'm deploying a live preview for you now." 


Workspace: A split screen appears with the generated code on one side and the live, rendered webpage on the other.

Phase 3: The "Launch Party" & Summary
The project is complete, and the user sees the full output of their "agency."


Gemini Live (Producer): "And with that, our campaign is complete. We've gone from a sketch to a full product launch in just a few minutes. All assets are available in your project brief." 

Workspace: The final summary page displays all assets generated by the agency:

Marketing Copy (Strategy Agent) 

Hero Image (Art Director Agent) 

Social Video (Video Producer Agent) 

Audio Jingle (Audio Agent) 

Podcast Ad (Audio Agent) 

Live Landing Page (Web Dev Agent) 

The "Wow": The user's experience is transformed. They are no longer just using an app; they are directing a powerful, autonomous creative agency.


The "How": The "Show Me the API" toggle  now reveals two levels of code:

The Gemini Live (WebSocket) code that shows the Producer managing the conversation.

The individual API calls (Gemini, Imagen, Veo, Lyria) that the Producer "delegated" to its specialist agents in the background.
